## Partial coverage

| Domain | Why it remains partial |
|---|---|
| Belief revision and truth maintenance | Foundational seed exists, but modern claim-graph implementations and benchmarks are sparse. |
| Temporal knowledge | Temporal KG papers and Graphiti exist, but stale-knowledge and temporal-revision benchmarks are incomplete. |
| Self-driving labs | Key papers and workflow tools are present; real reusable closed-loop experiment traces are rare. |
| Evaluator governance | Replication and benchmark material exists; evaluator independence, gaming, and replacement protocols need more evidence. |

## Materially under-covered

- probabilistic knowledge graphs and calibrated epistemic uncertainty;
- entity resolution, identity criteria, merge/split governance, and benchmarked recovery;
- causal, counterfactual, process, and intervention knowledge;
- units, measurements, instruments, calibration, and multimodal grounding;
- negative knowledge, defaults, exceptions, and defeasible reasoning;
- privacy, right to erasure, derived-data deletion, and access-policy propagation;
- multilingual ontology and multilingual citation fidelity;
- failed replications, negative results, retractions, and production incidents;
- institutional authority, scientific consensus, dissent, and jurisdiction;
- concurrent multi-agent editing and semantic merge conflicts;
- source-removal and dependency-aware wiki recompilation benchmarks.

## Coverage rule

A topic is not “covered” because one paper was added. Engineering-relevant coverage should contain, where appropriate:

1. conceptual or formal primary sources;
2. implementation;
3. dataset or benchmark;
4. operational or field evidence;
5. governance and failure material;
6. a current verification date and version.
