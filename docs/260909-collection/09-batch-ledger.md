
# 260909 Batch Ledger

## Batch intent

Build a broad, auditable source base for knowledge self-evolution, then close structural gaps in automated research, open implementations, ontology, and LLM Wiki.

## Major collection lanes

- knowledge, memory, temporal graph, and model editing;
- RSI and self-evolving agents;
- open-ended evolution and quality diversity;
- Auto Research and autonomous experimentation;
- evaluation, replication, and governance;
- GitHub implementations and infrastructure;
- continual learning, belief revision, and temporal knowledge;
- ontology, schema, mappings, operational semantics, policy, and provenance;
- LLM Wiki, Wikipedia-style governance, long-form synthesis, and wiki maintenance.

## Validation baseline

The repository has automated YAML/schema/reference validation. The LLM Wiki batch also adds documentation validation. A green CI result means the checked contracts and references are consistent; it does not mean the scientific corpus is complete or every claim has been independently verified.

## Known handoff blockers

- legacy metadata migration remains incomplete;
- most paper full text remains URL-only;
- dataset and benchmark objects are not yet first-class everywhere;
- claim/evidence instances and the actual wiki have not yet been built;
- evaluation gold sets and rollback drills are not yet present.
