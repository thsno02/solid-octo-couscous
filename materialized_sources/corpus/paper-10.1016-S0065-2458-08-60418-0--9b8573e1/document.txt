1. Introduction 
The survival of man depends on the early construction of an ultra- 
intelligent machine. 
In order to design an ultraintelligent machine we need to understand 
more about the human brain or human thought or both. In the follow- 
ing pages an attempt is made to take more of the magic out of the brain 
by means of a “subassembly” theory, which is a modification of Hebb’s 
famous speculative cell-assembly theory. My belief is that the first 
ultraintelligent machine is most likely to incorporate vast artificial 
neural circuitry, and that its behavior will be partly explicable in terms
